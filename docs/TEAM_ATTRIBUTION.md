# Team Attribution

Some ITAI 4371 work was completed collaboratively. This repository is organized to make authorship clear.

## Attribution rule

- Team-created material should be labeled as team work.
- Individual analysis, notes, code, diagrams, or presentation sections should be identified as personal contributions.
- No group deliverable should be presented as solely authored by one person unless that is accurate.

## My individual focus

My personal contribution centered on NVIDIA Isaac Sim for robotics and virtual testing, including physics-based simulation, sensor modeling, robot behavior, and simulation-to-real considerations.

## EMS / ambulance material

Ambulance and emergency-response digital twin concepts are included as examples or team-context use cases. They should not be described as a standalone individual ambulance digital twin unless a separate individual implementation is created later.
